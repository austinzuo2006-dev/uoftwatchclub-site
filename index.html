About

Who we are

  • Free membership
  • All UofT students welcome
  • St. George campus

The University of Toronto Watch Club (UTWC) brings together students who are interested in watches of every kind — mechanical, luxury and smart. Whether you own a collection or have never worn a watch, you are welcome here.

We explore watchmaking, watch history, design and wearable technology, and we take a close look at the watch market itself: pricing, the secondary market, and what data can tell us about both. Along the way, we connect students with each other and with people working in the watch and wearable technology industries.

Membership is free and open to all registered UofT students.

Meetups & visits

Casual get-togethers to share watches and talk shop, plus visits to watch shops and brand boutiques.

Speaker events

Talks with collectors, retailers, watchmakers and people in the watch and wearables industries.

Workshops

Hands-on sessions on movements, design, care and the basics of watchmaking.

Market & data

Digging into watch prices, the secondary market and the data behind them.
