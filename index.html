01 — About

We explore how watches are made, how they are designed, and how the market prices them. No collection or experience required.

The University of Toronto Watch Club (UTWC) brings together students who are interested in watches of every kind — mechanical, luxury and smart. Whether you own a collection or have never worn a watch, you are welcome here.

We study watchmaking, watch history, design and wearable technology, and we take a close look at the watch market itself: pricing, the secondary market, and what data can tell us about both. Along the way, we connect students with each other and with people working in the watch and wearable technology industries.
